Season pass operations

GRANT_SEASON_XP adds progress on the in-window seasonpass_ and converts it to levels. A missing season or a closed window does nothing. XP sources are triggerables, including ON_KILL. Do not add a kill world script, and do not hardcode the next month's rewards in the Backend. Ship a new seasonpass_ file, and quote start and end. SET_SEASON_TRACK stores FREE or PAID. A shop calls it from APPLY_TRANSACTION onsuccess. REDEEM_SEASON_REWARD stamps redeemed and runs that row's reward ops. Paid rows require PAID. OPEN_SEASON_PASS opens the page or the scroll. A closed window shows a barrier row. The season script is on season pass.

GRANT_SEASON_XP​

Task: operation_grant_season_xp. Adds progress on the in-window seasonpass_. Converts to levels with counted repeat. Missing season or closed window is a no-op. Non-players are skipped. XP sources are triggerables (ON_KILL smoke: triggerable_seasonpass_kill_xp). Do not add a kill world script.

KeyRequiredNotes
typeyesGRANT_SEASON_XP
amountnoXP to add. Default 1. Number or stat_*
seasonnoseasonpass_example or example. Omit: first in-window file
targetnoWho. Overlay ENTITY

Smoke: mechanic_example_seasonpass self_xp. /packseasonpass xp 10. Wiki: season-pass.md.

SET_SEASON_TRACK​

Task: operation_set_season_track. Persist FREE or PAID on that season. Shop APPLY_TRANSACTION onsuccess calls this.

KeyRequiredNotes
typeyesSET_SEASON_TRACK
trackyesFREE or PAID
seasonnoSame as GRANT_SEASON_XP
targetnoWho. Overlay ENTITY

Smoke: self_paid / self_free. /packseasonpass track paid.

REDEEM_SEASON_REWARD​

Task: operation_redeem_season_reward. Stamps redeemed then runs that row's rewards ops (GRANT_LOOT / GIVE_ITEM / SET_STAT). Paid rows require PAID. Omit level and track to mass redeem. Omit track with a level to claim both rows for that level.

KeyRequiredNotes
typeyesREDEEM_SEASON_REWARD
levelnoTrack row. Omit: every eligible row
tracknoFREE or PAID. Omit: both
seasonnoSame as GRANT_SEASON_XP
targetnoWho. Overlay ENTITY

Smoke: PAGE click, SCROLL row click, self_redeem_all, /packseasonpass redeem.

OPEN_SEASON_PASS​

Task: operation_open_season_pass. Opens menu_seasonpass (PAGE) or menu_seasonpass_scroll (SCROLL) with a Backend overlay. New menu id each open so PAGE recopies itemlist. Closed window shows a barrier row.

KeyRequiredNotes
typeyesOPEN_SEASON_PASS
viewnoPAGE (default) or SCROLL
menunoOverride the rules menu script
seasonnoSame as GRANT_SEASON_XP
targetnoWho. Overlay ENTITY

Smoke: hub slots, self_open / self_open_scroll, /packseasonpass / /packseasonpass scroll.
